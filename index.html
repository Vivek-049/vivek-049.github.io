01Experience

From research labs to a production workcell.

Robot learning research at the University of Minnesota, now training and deploying robot policies at Factory Intelligence.

  1. Aug 2026 – Present Lafayette, IN

    Robot Learning Intern

    Factory Intelligence

    • Deployed a robotic assembly workcell with two YAM arms for an electrical fabrication client, chaining task-specific robot policies via a learned completion flag, with high success rates over multi-hour production runs.
    • Mid-training π0.5 on 100+ h of multi-task YAM data to improve downstream fine-tuning, and applying human-in-the-loop RL to precise, contact-rich tasks such as screwing and wire hooking.
    • Fine-tuning π-family policies with a joint-torque history token, along with other robot policies, on 800+ episodes of contact-rich tasks on YAM arms and the Almond Axol, a wheeled semi-humanoid.
    • Contributed to FI-OS, the company's in-house robot-learning platform covering robot onboarding, teleoperation, data collection, training, evals and deployment.
